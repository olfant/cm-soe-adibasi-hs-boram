Holistic Growth

Student Life Beyond Classrooms

Co-curricular activities form a central pillar of holistic education at CM SoE ADIBASI H.S. BORAM. Students are encouraged to develop physical stamina, leadership, creative expression, and appreciation for traditional heritage through structured sports and cultural programs.

Athletics & Games

Sports Activities

Our sports program emphasizes team collaboration, fitness, and fair play. Students train under guided supervision for inter-house matches, district tournaments, and athletics meets.

  • Football team training and intra-school tournaments
  • Track and field events including sprints and relays
  • Annual Sports Meet with medals and certificates of merit
  • Physical fitness drills and morning assembly exercises
Arts & Heritage

Cultural Programs

Rooted in the vibrant adivasi and folk traditions of Jharkhand, our cultural programs celebrate music, dance, visual arts, and literary expression.

  • Traditional tribal and classical dance performances
  • Folk music, choral singing, and instrumental training
  • Art, craft, drawing, and exhibition displays
  • Debate, essay writing, and recitation competitions
Student Engagement

Student Participation

Every student is encouraged to participate in at least one co-curricular activity, sports event, or school committee. This builds self-confidence, camaraderie, and organizational leadership skills that prepare youth for higher education and civic life.

Annual Calendar

School Events

Major institutional events include the Annual Sports Meet (15th November 2025), National Days celebrations (Independence Day, Republic Day), Science Day exhibitions, and Teachers' Day programs organized with enthusiasm by students and teachers alike.
