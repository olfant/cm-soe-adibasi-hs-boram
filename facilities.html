School Facilities

Modern Infrastructure & Learning Spaces at CM SoE ADIBASI H.S. BORAM.

  1. Home
  3. Facilities
Campus Amenities

Modern Learning Infrastructure

CM SoE ADIBASI H.S. BORAM has upgraded facilities designed to inspire active student participation, digital literacy, scientific inquiry, and healthy physical recreation.

Digital Education

1. Smart Classrooms

Equipped with interactive digital teaching devices, audio-visual display systems, and ergonomic student seating. Smart classrooms transform traditional textbook topics into engaging visual multimedia lessons that enhance student retention and focus.

  • Interactive multimedia teaching displays
  • Well-ventilated, naturally lit lecture spaces
  • Comfortable dual desks for students
Digital Literacy

2. Computer Laboratory

A modern ICT-enabled computer laboratory designed to impart essential computer education, typing skills, digital tools usage, and basic coding fundamentals to students from Class VI through XII.

  • Modern computer workstations for hands-on student practice
  • Guided computer application curriculum
  • Internet-enabled research and learning environment
Hands-on Inquiry

3. Science & Learning Spaces

Equipped learning spaces for scientific demonstration, microscopes, chemical apparatus, biological specimens, and physics experiment kits. Students gain practical experience that reinforces classroom theory with direct experimental discovery.

  • Physics, Chemistry, and Biology apparatus and kits
  • Microscopes and scientific measurement instruments
  • Dedicated demonstration tables for teacher guidance
Knowledge Hub

4. School Library

A well-stocked institutional library offering a wide selection of curriculum textbooks, reference guides, Hindi and English literature, encyclopedias, and educational periodicals that nurture reading habits and independent study.

  • Extensive collection of academic reference books
  • Comfortable quiet reading tables and benches
  • Educational magazines, journals, and competitive guides
Physical Education

5. Sports Infrastructure

Physical health, sportsmanship, and teamwork are fundamental to student life at CM SoE Boram. The campus features an open outdoor playground for football, athletics, and team sports, supported by physical training instructors.

  • Open playground for football, track & field events
  • Equipment for team sports and athletic training
  • Annual Sports Meet organization and inter-school sports participation

CM SoE ADIBASI H.S. BORAM

A Centre of Excellence for Quality Education in Boram, Jharkhand. Dedicated to providing inclusive, modern, and holistic education to nurture students for future leadership.
