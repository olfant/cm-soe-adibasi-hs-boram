About School

CM School of Excellence Adibasi High School, Boram is a Government of Jharkhand institution functioning under the Department of School Education & Literacy. As part of the Chief Minister's School of Excellence initiative, the school has been developed as a Centre of Excellence to provide quality, inclusive and modern education to students of Boram and the surrounding rural and tribal regions.

The school offers education from Class VI to XII and is home to more than 800 students and over 35 teachers and staff members. Our aim is to give every child, regardless of background, access to the kind of learning environment and opportunities found in the best schools of the country.

Our campus is equipped with smart classrooms, a computer laboratory, science learning spaces, a well-stocked library and dedicated sports infrastructure. Teaching at the school focuses on concept-based and activity-based learning, regular assessment and individual attention, helping students build strong academic foundations along with practical and digital skills.

Beyond academics, the school places equal importance on holistic development. Through sports, cultural programs, clubs, school houses and the student cabinet, students develop confidence, creativity, teamwork and leadership qualities. At the same time, we take pride in the rich cultural heritage of Jharkhand and encourage our students to stay rooted in their values and traditions.

With the constant support of parents, the School Management Committee and the Department, CM SoE Adibasi H.S. Boram continues to grow with one guiding belief: Learning Today, Leading Tomorrow.

Offering education from Class VI to XII, the school blends strong academic foundations with modern learning tools: smart classrooms, a computer laboratory, science learning spaces, a well-stocked library and dedicated sports infrastructure. Our dedicated teachers focus on concept-based learning, curiosity and confidence, so that every child, especially those from tribal and rural backgrounds, gets an equal opportunity to excel.

Beyond the classroom, we nurture character, discipline and leadership through sports, cultural programs, clubs, school houses and the student cabinet, while taking pride in the rich cultural heritage of Jharkhand. With over 800 students and 35+ teachers and staff, CM SoE Adibasi H.S. Boram continues to live by its motto: Learning Today, Leading Tomorrow.

Methodology

Our Educational Approach

At CM SoE ADIBASI H.S. BORAM, learning extends beyond textbooks into practical application, interactive dialogue, and skill building.

Quality Education

Focusing on strong foundational concepts, interactive smart-board instruction, regular formative assessments, and individualized student guidance across all grades.

Student Development

Cultivating critical thinking, self-confidence, team cooperation, digital fluency, and cultural awareness through sports, cultural arts, and community engagement.

School Campus

The school is situated in Boram, Jharkhand, in a green and serene environment conducive to concentrated study, sports, and outdoor activities.
