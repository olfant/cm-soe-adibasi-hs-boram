Institutional Profile

School Overview

CM SoE Adivasi H.S. Boram is a CM School of Excellence under the Government of Jharkhand, dedicated to providing quality, inclusive and modern education.

Established as part of the flagship CM School of Excellence (E-Vidya Niketan) initiative by the Department of School Education & Literacy, Government of Jharkhand, the institution serves as a lighthouse educational center in Boram. The school provides state-of-the-art academic facilities, digital classrooms, and balanced co-curricular training for students across Classes VI through XII.

The school nurtures academic excellence, character, creativity and life skills among students, ensuring that quality secondary and higher secondary education is accessible to all communities in the region.

Our Vision

To be a premier Centre of Excellence in Jharkhand that empowers every student with contemporary knowledge, strong moral foundation, scientific temper, and leadership qualities to thrive in a dynamic global society.

Our Mission

To deliver inclusive, equitable, and quality school education through dedicated teaching, experiential learning spaces, modern labs, and comprehensive co-curricular programs that develop intellectual curiosity and societal responsibility.

Methodology

Our Educational Approach

At CM SoE ADIBASI H.S. BORAM, learning extends beyond textbooks into practical application, interactive dialogue, and skill building.

Quality Education

Focusing on strong foundational concepts, interactive smart-board instruction, regular formative assessments, and individualized student guidance across all grades.

Student Development

Cultivating critical thinking, self-confidence, team cooperation, digital fluency, and cultural awareness through sports, cultural arts, and community engagement.

Campus & Environment

School Campus

The school is situated in Boram, Jharkhand, in a green and serene environment conducive to concentrated study, sports, and outdoor activities.
