Academic Framework

Academic Overview

CM SoE ADIBASI H.S. BORAM offers a robust and structured educational curriculum for students from Class VI to Class XII under the Government of Jharkhand. Our curriculum integrates rigorous textbook mastery with modern smart-class digital instruction, laboratory experiments, and continuous assessment.

Our dedicated faculty members utilize student-centered teaching methodologies that foster conceptual clarity, intellectual discipline, and practical application of knowledge across science, mathematics, social sciences, and languages.

Grade Structure

Classes VI–XII Structure

The school accommodates 12 distinct class divisions spanning Upper Primary, Secondary, and Higher Secondary education:

Middle Stage (Classes VI–VIII)

Focus on strengthening foundational literacy, numeracy, experiential science, environmental studies, and communication skills.

Secondary Stage (Classes IX–X)

Focused academic instruction preparing students for secondary state board examinations, scientific experiments, and competitive readiness.

Higher Secondary (Classes XI–XII)

Specialized higher secondary studies preparing students for higher education, career pathways, and state/national examinations.

Pedagogy

Teaching and Learning

Our pedagogical philosophy is anchored in active classroom engagement. Teachers use multimedia smart boards, model demonstrations, interactive problem-solving sessions, and peer learning groups to ensure that every student grasps complex topics easily.

  • Interactive smart board lectures and digital visualizations
  • Hands-on laboratory investigations and experiment work
  • Remedial and personalized doubt-clearing sessions
  • Regular homework evaluation and continuous feedback
Standards

Academic Excellence

Academic excellence at CM SoE Boram is driven by structured academic calendars, continuous formative tests, student mentorship, and dedicated teacher training sponsored by the Department of School Education & Literacy.

  • Consistent monitoring of learning outcomes across all 12 classes
  • Focus on foundational science, mathematics, and language proficiency
  • Encouragement of student participation in state-level academic events
  • Regular parent-teacher interactions to support student progress
Assessments

Examination Information & Learning Environment

Examination Framework

Student assessments are conducted systematically in accordance with state education department schedules.

Assessment Schedule

Includes Unit Tests, Periodic Assessments, Half Yearly Examinations (Classes VI–XII), and Annual Final Board/Term Examinations as notified through official school notices.

Student Learning Environment

The school campus offers disciplined, calm, and well-ventilated classrooms with ergonomic desks, natural daylight, and a positive academic atmosphere that encourages curious questioning and focused study.
